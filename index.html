How It Works

No red tape, a fast yes or no

You work directly with the decision maker and a skilled underwriter, from pre-approval to payoff.

1. Pre-approval

Call or apply online. You do not need a specific property to get a general pre-approval.

2. Letter of intent

Once your contract is ratified, we issue a commitment on specific terms.

3. Close in 7 to 10 days

4. Draws and servicing

Funds in the construction escrow release as you hit your milestones.
